About Me

Hello! I'm Pimnaporn, an online Thai tutor based in Bangkok, GMT+7. I'm passionate about helping learners connect with the Thai language through real-life conversations and authentic communication.

My teaching philosophy centers on practical learning — you'll master not just grammar rules, but the expressions and politeness techniques that locals actually use in everyday situations. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to refine your fluency, I tailor each lesson to your level and goals.

How Lessons Work

Track 1: Romanization

Perfect for learners who can't read Thai script yet. We focus on natural pronunciation and conversation foundations using romanized Thai.

Track 2: Thai Script

For those ready to read Thai script. Build accuracy and fluency with authentic written Thai, advancing your language skills to the next level.

Each lesson is customized to your learning style and pace. We work together to ensure steady improvement and genuine confidence in speaking Thai.
